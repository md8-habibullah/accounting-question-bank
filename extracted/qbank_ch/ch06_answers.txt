14 Correct answer(s):
    A     2 and 4
    In preparing a bank reconciliation, uncredited lodgements reduce an overdrawn balance in the bank
    statement. If a payment received from a customer is subsequently dishonoured, a credit entry in the
    cash at bank account is required to reinstate the debt. Not all differences between the cash at bank
    account and the bank statement must be corrected by means of a journal entry, since some items
    appear in the bank reconciliation only (eg, errors by the bank, payments that have not cleared the
    bank account and uncredited lodgements). Bank charges not yet entered in the cash at bank account
    should be dealt with by updating the cash at bank account, not by making an adjustment to the
    balance per the bank statement.
