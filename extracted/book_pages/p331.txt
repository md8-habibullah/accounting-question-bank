3.3   Thereducingbalancemethodofdepreciation
Definition
Reducing balance depreciation:The annual depreciation charge is a fixed percentage ofthe
broughtforward carrying amount of the asset.
The reducing balance method might be used to allocate a greater proportion of the total
depreciable amounttotheasset's earlieryears anda lowerproportionto its lateryears,as the
benefits obtained bythe business from using the asset decline over time.
Note:When calculating teducing balancedepreciation in an exam youwill NOTbeconcerned
